What is real in here, and what is not

Real

  • · Every patient-visible string is looked up by content key through C(key) — the same shape as GET /content/{key}?lang=. Nothing is typed inline in a screen.
  • · The check-in runs the real placeholder-v1 tier rules. Answer calmly and you land on P11; tick “Chest pain” and P13 fires.
  • · The tier is computed in mockServer.postCheckin(), never in a screen — matching the rule that a client is never trusted with a safety decision.
  • · Consent stays disabled until you scroll to the end and tick the box. Try it.
  • · Turn on Offline: check-in is blocked and explicitly not queued, while tasks still tick.

Deliberate deviations — your call

  • · The emergency instruction is a persistent red button, not a full-width bar. The spec asks for a 44dp bar carrying the whole approved string — but that string is 103 characters and wraps to three lines at 360dp, so the bar could never actually be 44 tall. The button is on every screen and not dismissible; tapping it opens the full verbatim text and the dial actions. The trade: the words are one tap away instead of always on screen. Content owner's call.
  • · Brand colour is blue, not the spec's teal #0F5F6B — matched to your clinician dashboard and the references you sent.
  • · Radii and shadows are softer than the spec's flat 12px cards, for the iOS feel.
  • · Safety tokens untouched: emergency #B3261E, urgent #B36B00, success #1B7F5A, overdue grey. Red appears only on the banner and P13.
  • · Clinical prose (P7, P15, P11 body) shows a content slot — no medical text is invented anywhere in this file.
  • · UI is English only; UZ and RU arrive from the content library after native-speaker review.
