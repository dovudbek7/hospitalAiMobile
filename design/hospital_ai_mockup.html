What to check while reviewing

  • · Overdue tasks are grey, never red — red appears only on the banner and P13.
  • · Every tier carries an icon + text label, never colour alone.
  • · Disabled buttons are visible (border fill + muted text), never ghosted away.
  • · P11 contains zero reassurance; P12 contains zero time promise.
  • · Percentages always show their denominator.
  • · Key actions are 64dp; the emergency call button is full-width 72dp.

One thing the spec cannot satisfy as written

The design system fixes the emergency banner at 44dp tall. The approved emergency.banner string — “Your clinic’s instruction: in an emergency, call 103. Do not use this app to report urgent symptoms.” — is 103 characters, which wraps to three lines at 14sp inside a 360dp screen, so it needs roughly 63dp. At 200% font scale it needs far more.

Every frame above renders it at min-height: 44dp and lets it grow, because truncating clinician-approved emergency text is not an option. Three ways out — the content owner picks:

  1. 1. Approve a short banner variant (e.g. “In an emergency, call 103”) and keep 44dp fixed.
  2. 2. Change the spec: banner is min 44dp and grows with its content — what is drawn here.
  3. 3. Banner shows the short line and expands to the full text on tap, before dialling.
